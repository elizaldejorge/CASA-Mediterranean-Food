Mi casa
es su casa.

CASA is Puerto Aventuras' home for "real food" — made with the highest-quality products, to eat in or take home.

A family affair with only a handful of tables and two big hearts behind every dish. Chef Nima and his daughter Clara welcome you to their open-air kitchen, where the ocean breeze drifts through by day and the warm night air settles in by evening. No doors, no fuss — just honest cooking and good company.

We serve breakfast, lunch and dinner every day except Mondays, and we're famous for Mediterranean-style tapas, a thoughtfully curated natural wine bar, juicy French-style rotisserie chickens, and daily specials.
